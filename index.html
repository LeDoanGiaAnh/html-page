3 Most Popular Coffee Beans

Coffee is more than just a drink; it is an art from farm to cup. Here are the most popular bean varieties:

  • Arabica: Known for its sweet, complex flavor profile, high acidity, and lower caffeine content.
  • Robusta: Strong, bold flavor with earthy notes, lower acidity, and twice the caffeine content of Arabica.
  • Liberica: A rare variety featuring a unique fruity, floral aroma and a bold, smoky flavor profile.
